Post-quantum verifiable credentials

Your degree shouldn’t expire when RSA does.

QChain signs credentials with ML-DSA-44, the NIST post-quantum standard, and anchors them on Hyperledger Fabric. Universities issue from QPortal. Graduates carry them in QWallet.

How it works

One credential, start to finish.

01 Issue

The university signs it.

A registrar fills in the credential in QPortal. Each field is hashed with its own salt, the set is signed with ML-DSA-44, and the commitment is written to Fabric.

SHA3-256 · ML-DSA-44 · Fabric 2.5

02 Receive

Only your phone can open it.

The full credential is encrypted to a key that was generated on your phone and never left it. QWallet pulls the ciphertext from IPFS and decrypts it locally.

ML-KEM-768 + AES-256-GCM · IPFS

03 Disclose

Share three fields, not six.

Choose what the verifier gets. Everything else stays behind as a salted hash that still adds up to the signed commitment. The switches on the phone work, try them.

Per-field salted commitments

04 Verify

Checked against the ledger, not a database.

Your phone signs a single-use QR that dies after 60 seconds. The verifier checks the issuer's signature, holder's signature, the on-chain commitment and revocation. One mismatch and it fails.

Two ML-DSA-44 signatures · fail-closed

About

An undergraduate capstone, built in the open.

Team QChain is a senior-year engineering group. Our project is titled Post-Quantum Cryptography in Blockchain Technology: A Quantum-Resistant Credential Verification System.

What we care about

01

Measure it

We run ML-DSA and ML-KEM inside a permissioned ledger and measure what they do to throughput and storage, instead of assuming.

02

Show the work

The source, the Docker Compose network, the test suites and our own security audit are public, so the results can be reproduced.

03

Protect what lasts

A degree outlives the cryptography it was signed with. We design for an attacker who records today and decrypts later.

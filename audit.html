Where QChain is strong, and where it isn’t yet.

We reviewed the Go backend, the Fabric chaincode, the Nginx gateway, the MySQL schema and both Flutter clients. All 25 findings are below.

None of the open findings is a weakness in the cryptography. They are ordinary web-service problems with ordinary fixes, and the two critical ones are first in line.

Posture

Against the six classic properties.

Integrity

Strong

SHA3-256 commitments are rebuilt from ledger state. Verification fails closed. IPFS CIDs are part of what is signed.

Confidentiality

Strong

Off-chain bodies are encrypted with ML-KEM-768. Selective disclosure is cryptographic, not cosmetic.

Authenticity

Improving

Issuer and holder signatures are ML-DSA-44. Fabric node certificates are still classical ECDSA.

Authentication

Critical gap

The backend API has no session or JWT middleware and no CORS restrictions. Staff routes are exposed.

Non-repudiation

Substantial

Both issuance and presentation are signed. A holder cannot deny having presented.

Availability

Demo-grade

One VM, one orderer, one peer per organisation, one IPFS node. Production needs a cluster.

Roadmap

The order we fix things in.

NowClose the authentication holes
  • Remove POST /dev/cleanupTest from the production router.F21
  • Lock bindHolderKeys. Reject overwrites once keys are registered.F22
  • Authenticate the API. JWT middleware and role checks on every privileged route.F01
  • Rate-limit OTP redemption on /resolveSession, with lockout.F03
  • Protect holder and staff endpoints behind authenticated sessions.F23 · F24
NextKey custody and hardening
  • Move the issuer signing key out of .env into an HSM or cloud KMS.F06
  • Make session redemption atomic to close the replay race.F12
  • Cap request bodies with http.MaxBytesReader.F10
  • Harden the gateway and encrypt PII. HSTS, CSP, rate limits; column encryption in MySQL.F07 · F14
LaterQuantum-safe end to end
  • ML-DSA for Fabric itself. Replace ECDSA in BCCSP, peer MSP certificates and TLS.F04
  • High availability. Multi-node Raft ordering, redundant peers, an IPFS cluster.F18
  • Anchor audit logs on-chain so they are tamper-evident.F13

A credential system asks to be trusted. Publishing what is still broken is part of earning that.
